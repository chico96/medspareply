# Local SEO + GBP prompt pack

> SpaReply Med Spa Review + Local SEO Toolkit · editable copy
> Clinic: [Practice Name] · City: [City]

Thirteen Google Business Profile post angles and a short prompt pack for the
treatment pages on your site. Pair each GBP post with one real, non-stock
image of your treatment area, your team, or your front desk. No patient
images, no before/after that names a guest.

---

## Cadence

- **GBP posts:** 1–2 per week. Don't bunch them on one day.
- **Treatment-page edits:** one page per month, paired with the GBP post that links to it.
- **Photo refresh:** three new clinic photos per month, descriptive filenames.

## 13 GBP post angles

Spring glow plan in [City]: Hydrafacial + LED finishing — three weekly slots

### 2. Pre-event prep
Wedding, reunion, or photoshoot on the calendar? Our practice manager can

### 3. Membership spotlight

### 4. Provider spotlight (no clinical claims)

### 6. FAQ · what to expect at a first consult

## Treatment-page outline · copy/paste

Use this outline for each treatment page on the [Practice Name] site. The
goal is to answer the questions a [City] guest already asks in reviews and
in consult calls.

1. **What it is, in plain language** — one paragraph, no marketing.
2. **Who it's a fit for** — three to five short bullets, written as
   guest-facing language ("you" not "patients").
3. **What to expect at the visit** — appointment length, comfort level,
   downtime, follow-up cadence.
4. **Pricing range** — give a range, not "starting at." If you offer a
   membership, name what it changes.
5. **Provider notes** — which providers at [Practice Name] perform this,
   and how to book with a specific one.
6. **FAQ** — six questions pulled from real review responses and consult
   notes. Plain answers, no clinical claims.
7. **Internal links** — link to the membership page, two related
   treatment pages, and the consult-booking page.
8. **GBP linkback** — the treatment page should be linked from the GBP
   services list and from at least one GBP post per quarter.

## City / neighborhood angles

If you serve more than one neighborhood, write one short page per area —
not a thin "[Practice Name] [neighborhood]" copy-paste. Each page should
answer:

- **Who you serve there** — the typical guest profile in plain language.
- **Where you are** — landmark, parking, transit. No address only.
- **Provider hours for that area** — if certain providers are
  in-clinic on certain days.
- **What's specific to that neighborhood** — a memberships angle, a local
  partnership, a specific pre-event prep cadence (wedding venues,
  conference hotels, photographers).

---

## What not to write on a GBP post or treatment page

- No before/after photos of named guests.
- No "results last [N] months" outcome claims unless your providers
  approved the wording.
- No "FDA-approved" language for off-label uses.
- No "the only [X] in [City]" language unless you can prove it on Monday.
- No staff scope language ("our top injector") on the GBP post — keep it
  on the treatment page where it can be qualified.
