# Operating cadence — print and tape next to the front desk

> SpaReply Med Spa Review + Local SEO Toolkit · editable copy
> Clinic: [Practice Name] · Last reviewed: [Date]

The whole point of the toolkit is that review work fits inside this cadence
— not the other way around. Drop this into your shared calendar, your SOP
wiki, or a printed sheet next to the front desk.

---

## Daily · 10 minutes — Front-desk lead

- Pull yesterday's new reviews from Google Business Profile.
- Draft replies for 4- and 5-star reviews into the shared doc.
- Flag anything 3 stars or below for [Practice Manager Name] — do not
  draft those publicly until cleared.
- Add each draft as a new row in the tracker (status: drafted).

---

## Daily · 15 minutes — Practice manager

- Approve the 4–5 star drafts the front-desk lead prepared.
- Post the approved replies, capture the public-reply link in the
  tracker, mark the row "posted."
- Personally draft (or co-draft) every 1–2 star reply with the front-desk
  lead before posting.
- Send the private follow-up email or call for any 1–2 star review.

---

## Tuesday · 20 minutes — Front-desk lead

Run the weekly SOP block:

1. Triage anything that's still open from the past 7 days.
2. Draft replies for any 4–5 star reviews that haven't been touched.
3. Run the 7-question safety check on each.
4. Post the approved replies, log to the tracker.
5. Scan the last 7 days for one signal — a repeating compliment, a
   repeating concern, or a new staff name being praised — and bring it
   to Friday's huddle.

---

## Friday · 10 minute huddle — Owner / practice manager / front-desk lead

- Share the one repeating compliment and one repeating concern from this
  week's reviews.
- Decide one operational tweak for next week. Don't try to fix
  everything — pick one thing the team can actually move on.
- Confirm coverage for the daily and Tuesday slots next week.
- Note the decision in the tracker (a "huddle-decisions" tab is fine).

---

## Monthly · 30 minutes — Practice manager

Roll up the tracker into a one-page summary:

- Total reviews this month, by star rating.
- Reviews by category: clinical, operational, billing, hostile, praise.
- Reviews by provider (named only when the guest named them publicly).
- One repeating compliment and one repeating concern.
- One operational change being made next month.
- Status of any open private follow-ups.

Share the one-pager with [Owner Name] and the providers. Keep the file in
the toolkit Drive folder for [Practice Name].

---

## Quarterly · 60 minutes — Owner

- Re-read the HIPAA-aware reply rules and the negative-review checklist.
- Refresh placeholders in the template bank for any new providers,
  services, or pricing changes since the last review.
- Review the GBP profile: services list, attributes, hours, photos.
- Pick the next quarter's headline metric (calls, direction requests,
  searches, conversion to booked consult) and write the target down.
- Confirm the toolkit Drive folder still has the latest version of every
  doc — the front desk shouldn't be working from a stale copy.
