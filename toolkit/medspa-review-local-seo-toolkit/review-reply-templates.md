# Review Reply Templates — Source Library

Twenty paste-ready public reply templates across ratings, services, and tones.
Designed for med spas. HIPAA-aware. No outcome promises.

**Placeholders:** `[guest first name]`, `[provider]`, `[service]`, `[clinic name]`,
`[practice manager email]`. Replace before posting. If the guest did not name
the provider or treatment publicly, leave those fields out.

**Universal rules:**

1. Mirror only details the guest already wrote publicly.
2. Never confirm protected health information (treatment specifics, medications,
   conditions, outcomes the guest did not state).
3. Never promise medical or cosmetic outcomes — even on 5★.
4. Move sensitive details to a private channel (`[practice manager email]`).
5. Keep replies under ~70 words for 5★, ~90 for 1–2★, ~60 for everything else.

## What to swap, and what not to

**Always swap:** the placeholders, the tone (warm / polished / clinical) to
match your brand, and the closing line to match your CTA.

**Never swap in:**

- A medication, dosage, syringe count, treatment plan, or outcome the guest
  did not already write publicly
- A provider's name unless the guest already named them publicly
- A guarantee, a "definitely won't happen again," or a clinical promise
- Emoji-stuffed corporate enthusiasm — it reads as inauthentic on aesthetic
  reviews and undermines the premium tone

The full toolkit ships this 20-template bank with safety rules, negative-review
scripts, GBP prompts, the 4-week content calendar, and operating cadence files
so the front desk can turn the examples into a repeatable weekly system.
