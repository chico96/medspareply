# Google Business Profile Prompt Pack

GBP post prompts and angles built from the questions med-spa guests actually
ask in reviews. Each prompt is a starting line — your team adds the local
detail (city, neighborhood, provider, season, slot count).

> **Posting cadence:** 1–2 GBP posts per week is the sustainable target.
> More than that and the post fades into the feed; less and the listing
> looks dormant. Pair posts with the 4-week content calendar so you never
> sit down to a blank page.

> **Reminder:** Treat GBP posts as public guest-visible surfaces. Apply the
> same HIPAA-aware checklist before publishing — no treatment specifics, no
> outcome promises, no protected health detail.

---

## Format reminder for every post

- **Headline:** ~60 characters max so it doesn't truncate on mobile
- **Body:** 150–250 words — guests scan, they don't read
- **One image** that is your own (not stock from a manufacturer's site)
- **One CTA button:** Call / Book / Learn more — pick one
- **One specific local hook:** city, neighborhood, season, slot count, event

## Image guidelines

- Use real clinic, real team, real rooms — not stock or supplier-provided
  imagery (which is shared across hundreds of clinics and underperforms).
- Avoid before/after photos in GBP posts. They invite outcome questions
  and complicate the privacy posture.
- Consistent crop and color profile across posts — pick one preset and
  stick to it for at least a quarter.

## Headline patterns that work

- "[City] guests asked us about [topic]. Here's the honest answer."
- "[N] [service] slots open this [day] in [neighborhood]."
- "What we [will / will not] do at [clinic name]."
- "Three things changed at [clinic name] this [season]."
- "[Service] in [city]: the [duration] visit, end to end."
