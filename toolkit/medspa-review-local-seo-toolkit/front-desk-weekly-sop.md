# Front-Desk Weekly Review SOP

A 20-minute weekly cadence for review work. One owner, one slot on the
calendar, one tracker. The goal: no review goes unanswered past Friday,
and no 1- or 2-star review goes out the door without a second pair of eyes.

> Save this as a recurring meeting on the front-desk lead's calendar:
> **Tuesday, 10:00–10:20 AM, "SpaReply review block."**

---

## Roles

- **Front-desk lead (owner of the SOP):** runs the 20-minute block, pulls
  the week's reviews, drafts replies, logs the tracker.
- **Practice manager (escalation owner):** approves any 1- or 2-star public
  reply before it posts, owns private follow-ups, escalates to owner.
- **Owner (rare-touch):** reviews monthly summary, approves refunds above
  the pre-set threshold, signs off on responses to clinical or legal
  flags.

If your team is two people, one person plays both first roles. Keep the
"two pairs of eyes" rule for 1- and 2-star reviews.

---

## The 20-minute block

### Minute 0–3 — Pull the week

Open Google Business Profile, Yelp, and any other platform you monitor.
Pull every review posted since the last block (default: 7 days).

For each review, note in a draft sheet:

- Source
- Star rating
- Guest name (as displayed)
- Review date and timestamp
- One-line summary

### Minute 3–10 — Draft public replies (5★ + 4★ + 3★)

Use `review-reply-templates.md`. For each review:

1. Pick the template that matches the rating + service + tone.
2. Replace placeholders.
3. Run the 7-question safety check from
   `hipaa-aware-safety-checklist.md`.
4. Paste the reply into the platform's reply box but **do not click post
   yet** if it's a 1- or 2-star review.

### Minute 10–14 — Draft 1- and 2-star public replies

Use `negative-review-playbook.md` section A.

- Always default to script A1 unless the review fits a more specific
  scenario (A2–A6).
- Tag the practice manager in your team chat. Do not post until they
  approve.
- Schedule the private follow-up (section B1) for within 1 business day.

### Minute 14–17 — Post and log

- Post all approved 5/4/3-star replies.
- Wait for practice manager approval before posting any 1/2-star reply.
- Log every review in the weekly tracker (template below).

### Minute 17–20 — One signal, one improvement

Look at the week's reviews collectively. Answer one question:

> *"Is there a theme three or more guests mentioned this week — positive or
> negative — that we should turn into a GBP post, an aftercare update, or
> an internal training note?"*

If yes, write it on a sticky note and bring it to Friday's team huddle.
If no, you're done.

## The weekly tracker (one row per review)

Copy this into Google Sheets or your team wiki. The Sheet version ships
in the purchase toolkit.

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Date posted | Public review date |
| Source | Google / Yelp / Facebook |
| Rating | 1–5 |
| Guest name | As publicly displayed |
| Service mentioned | If named publicly |
| Tone of review | Warm / neutral / negative / hostile |
| Reply drafted by | Front-desk lead name |
| Reply approved by | Practice manager (required for 1–2★) |
| Reply posted | Y / N + timestamp |
| Private follow-up | Y / N + channel |
| Private outcome | Resolved / pending / no response / escalated |
| Theme tag | One word — e.g. "wait time", "aftercare", "membership" |

---

## Monthly roll-up (15 minutes, first Tuesday of the month)

Once a month, the practice manager reviews:

- **Volume:** total reviews / month, trend vs. prior month
- **Average rating:** by source, by service if visible
- **Median time to public reply:** target ≤ 24 business hours for 5★,
  ≤ 48 for everything else
- **Top three themes** from the theme-tag column
- **Open private follow-ups** carried into the next month
- **One operational change** to bring to the next ops meeting

Email the roll-up to the owner. Three sentences plus the table is enough.

---

## Escalation tree

Use this when a review crosses out of the normal cadence. Do not improvise.

```
Front desk
   ↓ (any 1/2★ review, or 3★ flagging clinical concern)
Practice manager
   ↓ (allegation of harm, regulatory mention, refund > $200, repeat hostile)
Owner + counsel
```

Until escalation closes, no public reply goes live. Acknowledge internally,
leave the public review unanswered, and log the timestamp of the
escalation.

---

## What good looks like after 4 weeks

- 100% of reviews answered within target time
- Zero 1- or 2-star replies posted by a single person
- A weekly theme that turns into either a GBP post, an aftercare tweak,
  or a training note
- A tracker that you can hand the owner in 30 seconds when they ask
  "how's reputation?"

If the front-desk lead is hitting these on autopilot after a month, the
SOP is working. If they're not, the bottleneck is almost always that the
20-minute block isn't actually on the calendar — fix that first.
